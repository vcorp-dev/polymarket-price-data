# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, useful place for developers and researchers.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Respect different levels of experience and technical backgrounds.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect credentials, customer information, and private data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and disclosure.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through [DepthFeed contact](https://depthfeed.com/contact). Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
